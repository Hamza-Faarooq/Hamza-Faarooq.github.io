Collaboration with NASA

S² manifoldsHEALPix ~196K nodesL_max = 51140× compute reduction

Curvature-Weighted Gradient Diversity: A Noise Measure for Geometry-Adaptive SGD
Independent Research
Preprint on arXiv · Submitted to TMLR (under review)
Mar 2026 – May 2026
Strongly-convex quadraticsHutchinson estimation~20% lower suboptimalityNoise-adaptive schedules
Developed Curvature-Weighted Gradient Diversity (CWGD), a noise measure that adjusts for landscape geometry by down-weighting high-curvature directions. Proved that CWGD-modulated cosine annealing reduces asymptotic suboptimality floors by a factor of 1/(1+α), with ~20% empirical improvement over plain cosine annealing across condition numbers and noise regimes. Resolved estimator degeneracies, delivering a provably exact Hutchinson-based implementation for diagonal Hessian approximations.

Criticality-Constrained Pruning for Energy-Efficient Spiking Neural Networks
Research Intern
University of Oulu · Prof. Shuai Lee
Mar 2025 – Jun 2025
90% sparsity95.6% MNIST accuracy73% energy reductionsnnTorch · CVXPY
Designed Criticality-Constrained Quadratic Pruning (CQP), a native PyTorch pipeline fusing surrogate-gradient criticality with weight magnitude to prune SNNs without convex solvers. Characterised a continuous-relaxation trap in QP-based pruning that causes a 44 pp accuracy collapse, and a zombie-weight failure mode in Adam fine-tuning. Combining weight sparsification with KL-divergence temporal analysis yields a 73% energy reduction at 70% sparsity, retaining 95.6% MNIST accuracy at 90% sparsity. Submitted to Neurocomputing (Elsevier).

ApexSearch: Audio-Visual RAG & Multimodal Video Retrieval Engine

2026
PyTorchFAISSMultimodal RAGStreamlitCLIP · CLAPGroq

An end-to-end multimodal RAG system for natural-language querying and live commentary over untrimmed Formula 1 broadcasts.

  • Localises events (crashes, pit stops) in untrimmed broadcasts from natural-language queries.
  • Extracts visual features at 1 FPS plus synchronised audio chunks, embedded with frozen CLIP and CLAP into separate FAISS indexes for sub-50ms retrieval.
  • Uses a dynamically weighted late-fusion score to resolve cross-modal ambiguity, for example trusting audio over visuals under heavy motion blur.
  • Deployed serverlessly on Streamlit Community Cloud, with Vision-Language Model inference delegated to Groq API calls for zero-cost hosting.
