Curvature-Weighted Gradient Diversity: A Noise Measure for Geometry-Adaptive SGD
Independent Research
Preprint out on arXiv
Submission to TMLR · Under Review
March 2026 – May 2026
Strongly-convex quadratics Hutchinson estimation 20% lower suboptimality Noise-adaptive schedules
Developed Curvature-Weighted Gradient Diversity (CWGD), a novel noise measure that adjusts for landscape geometry by down-weighting high-curvature parameter directions. Proved that CWGD-modulated cosine annealing reduces asymptotic suboptimality floors by a factor of 1/(1+α), validated by empirical improvements of ~20% over plain cosine annealing across various condition numbers and noise regimes. Identified and resolved estimator degeneracies, delivering a provably exact Hutchinson-based implementation for diagonal Hessian approximations.

Criticality-Constrained Pruning for Energy-Efficient Spiking Neural Networks
Research Intern
University of Oulu · Prof. Shuai Lee
Mar 2025 – Jun 2025
90% sparsity 95.6% MNIST accuracy 73% energy reduction CQP · CVXPY · snnTorch MNIST · Fashion-MNIST
Designed Criticality-Constrained Quadratic Pruning (CQP), a native PyTorch pipeline fusing surrogate-gradient criticality with weight magnitude to prune SNNs without convex solvers. Formally characterised a continuous-relaxation trap in QP-based pruning causing 44 pp accuracy collapse, and identified a zombie-weight failure mode in Adam fine-tuning. Combined weight sparsification with KL-divergence temporal analysis yields a compound 73% energy reduction at 70% sparsity, retaining 95.6% MNIST accuracy at 90% sparsity. Submitted to Neurocomputing (Elsevier).

ApexSearch: Audio-Visual RAG & Multimodal Video Retrieval Engine

2026
PyTorch & Transformers FAISS Multimodal RAG Streamlit
CLIP & CLAP Late-Fusion Heuristic Groq LPU Inference

Engineered an interactive, end-to-end multimodal retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) system allowing natural language querying and autonomous live commentary over untrimmed, high-speed Formula 1 broadcasts.

  • Localizes specific events (e.g., crashes, pit stops) in untrimmed sports broadcasts from natural language queries.
  • Extracts temporal visual features (1 FPS) and synchronized audio chunks, vectorizing them via frozen CLIP and CLAP transformers into independent FAISS databases for sub-50ms retrieval.
  • Implements a dynamically weighted late-fusion scoring heuristic to resolve cross-modal ambiguity (e.g., trusting audio over visual during heavy motion blur).
  • Serverless deployment on GitHub and Streamlit Community Cloud, with Vision-Language Model inference offloaded to Groq API calls for zero-cost hosting.
